**Typography System:** Display: "Space Grotesk" (700 weight, uppercase, tracked). Body: "IBM Plex Mono" (400 weight). The contrast between grotesque display and monospace body reinforces the industrial-digital duality.

2. Functional color — used only to direct attention, never decoratively

**Color Philosophy:** Warm white (#FAFAF8) background, near-black (#1A1A1A) text, and a single deep forest green (#1B4332) accent. Green evokes growth, reliability, and precision — the qualities of a trusted technical partner.

**Layout Paradigm:** Strict 12-column grid with deliberate column-spanning. The hero uses a 7/5 column split — large text left, visual right. Service cards use a 3-column grid. No full-bleed sections except the footer.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin rule lines used as structural dividers and list markers
2. Small-caps labels in forest green above every section heading

**Interaction Philosophy:** Restrained and purposeful. Hover states use color shifts rather than motion. The interface communicates confidence through stillness.

**Animation:** Subtle fade-in on scroll. No parallax. Smooth underline draw on link hover. Page transitions use a clean opacity fade.

**Typography System:** Display: "Fraunces" (700, italic for emphasis). Body: "Libre Baskerville" (400). The serif pairing signals authority and craftsmanship — unusual in tech, which makes it distinctive.

## Selected Approach
**Chosen: Dark Futurism / Cyberpunk Editorial (Response 2)**

This approach best communicates AutomataForge's identity as a cutting-edge AI automation company. The deep navy and neon accent palette signals technical sophistication, the bento grid layout creates visual interest, and the glassmorphism cards feel premium and modern. This design will stand out dramatically on Fiverr/Upwork profiles and make the course offering feel like a premium product.
